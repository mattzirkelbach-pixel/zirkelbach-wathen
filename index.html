Four families, one marriage.

The Zirkelbachs, Dailys, Wathens and Vogts came together in Evansville, Indiana, when Donald Zirkelbach married Cleo Wathen in 1930. This is who they were, where they came from, and what the records say.

Police work runs through the family. Bena's father, Charles Vogt, was a township constable and justice of the peace. Pete Zirkelbach joined the Evansville police in the 1920s, and his son Donald followed in 1934. After them came Donald's son John and his grandson Adam.
