Four families,
one marriage.

A Bavarian baker's son, an Irish farmhand who landed with ten dollars, a Maryland carpenter under indenture, a Hessian family fleeing the hungry forties. Their lines crossed in Evansville, Indiana, when Donald Zirkelbach married Cleo Wathen on 5 April 1930.

Three centuries, four countries, fifteen places, and what the records say about each of them.

Follow the journeysOpen the tree

None of the four names carries a verified coat of arms. Arms were granted to individuals, not surnames, and the "family crests" sold online are inventions. The marks above are drawn from what each name means and what its first bearer here did.
